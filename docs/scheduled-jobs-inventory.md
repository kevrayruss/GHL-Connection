## Summary (plain English)

- Almost nothing in the old system ran by itself. It relied on Kevin starting a chat and saying "do the next batch".
- There was **no cron, no launchd, no Mac login item, no Zapier and no Make.com** automation. n8n was listed but it failed to connect ("404") every time it was tried in September.
- **Only one real scheduled job ever worked.** It was a Claude Cowork scheduled task called `streamline-inbox-ingest`. It ran **hourly at :09 on the office Mac** in June 2026. It copied finished ChatGPT page copy from Google Docs into the Streamline build folder. It fired once without help (11 June, 14:09 UTC). After that it hit permission prompts and was switched to "manual only".
- Three other Cowork scheduled tasks were one-time tests on the same evening (10 June). The content test worked. The two "build the website pages" tests failed because they needed someone to click permission dialogs. The old notes say scheduled builds "are NOT viable".
- The Personal OS plan (6 Sept) designed a **7:00 morning brief, a 4:30 afternoon close, a nightly capture sweep and a Friday review**. **None of these were ever built.** The plan itself says "zero scheduled tasks exist". Kevin said "hold off" on the database.
- Two things *do* repeat outside Claude. They run inside paid SEO tools, not on the Mac:
  - **Local Brand Manager** runs a monthly GeoGrid scan, report and audit for RLC. One of its two keywords, "lawn care", is wrong. It uses 169 credits a month.
  - **GMB Crush GBP Guard** watches the RLES profile.
- A lot of the rest is "run it when you need it" material: 5 document engines, calculators, QA checkers, one website checker and one Apps Script helper. These are worth keeping as reference.
- **About 90 scripts were one-off builders** for single documents. Keep them with their projects as history. Do not rebuild them.
- In n8n cloud today, the only scheduled workflow is "Handoff Inbox Doc (hourly)". None of the old jobs have been rebuilt yet.

**What to rebuild first in n8n, if anything:** (1) the Personal OS morning brief, read-only (it was designed but never built), and (2) a weekly job that reads the Local Brand Manager results (after a person fixes the wrong keyword). Everything else can wait or be retired.
